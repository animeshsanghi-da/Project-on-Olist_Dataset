## Questions likely to be asked by the company stakeholders from Animesh (Data Analyst)
---

### 1. Sales & Revenue Trends
1. **Monthly Growth:** What is the monthly revenue, and when do sales peak?
2. **Peak Traffic:** Which days and hours get the most orders?
3. **Average Spending:** What is the Average Order Value (AOV) per year?

### 2. Logistics & Delivery Performance
1. **Delivery Time:** What is the average delivery time, and which states have the slowest deliveries?
2. **Late Rates:** How many orders are delivered late?
3. **Shipping Costs:** Does high shipping cost cause order cancellations?

### 3. Customer Buying Behavior
1. **Payment Methods:** What are the most used payment methods, and do credit cards lead to higher spending?
2. **Installments:** Do payment installments increase order value?
3. **Top Spenders:** Which 10 cities have the highest-spending customers?

### 4. Product Catalog Insights
1. **Top Products:** Which product categories generate the most revenue and most sales?
2. **Late Penalty:** How much do late deliveries lower customer review scores?
3. **Listing Quality:** Do more photos and longer descriptions improve product review scores?

### 5. Predictive Analytics
1. **Predicting Satisfaction:** Can a Linear Regression model predict how much review scores drop per day of delay?

## Questions to be answered by the Data Analyst to the company stakeholders in the project by adding Problem Definition & Key Analytical factors.
---

### 1. Sales & Revenue Trends (The Bottom Line)
1. **MoM Growth:**
What is the total revenue generated month-over-month, and are there distinct seasonal spikes (e.g., holiday seasons)?
2. **Peak Traffic:**
Which day of the week (purchase_day_of_week) and hour of the day (purchase_hour) see the highest volume of successful orders? (Actionable for timing marketing blasts or flash sales).
3. **AOV (Average Order Value):**
What is the average amount a customer spends per transaction, and how does this change over the years?

### 2. Logistics & Operational Efficiency
1. **Delivery Baselines:**
What is the average delivery_time_days across the platform, and which specific states (customer_state) suffer from the slowest average deliveries?
2. **The Cost of Delay:**
What percentage of total orders are flagged as is_late_delivery?
3. **Freight Impact:**
How does the freight_ratio (the cost of shipping relative to the item price) affect purchasing behavior? Do higher freight ratios lead to more canceled orders?

### 3. Customer Behavior & Segmentation
1. **Payment Preferences:**
What is the distribution of payment_type? Do customers using credit cards have a significantly higher AOV than those using other methods?
2. **Installment Psychology:**
Does the availability of payment_installments correlate with customers buying more expensive items (total_item_value)?
3. **High-Value Customers (RFM Prep):**
Which top 10 cities have the highest concentration of customers in the top 5% based on total payment value?

### 4. Product Catalog & Satisfaction
1. **Volume vs. Value:**
Which product_category_name drives the highest total revenue, and which drives the highest volume of individual items sold?
2. **The Late Penalty:**
Is there a direct, quantifiable drop in the average review_score when an order's delivery_delay_days is greater than 0?
3. **Listing Quality:**
Do products with longer descriptions (product_description_lenght) or more photos (product_photos_qty) statistically receive higher review scores or lower return rates?

### 5. Predictive Analytics & Future Impact
1. **The Late Penalty Model:**
Can we mathematically predict the exact drop in customer satisfaction for every additional day a delivery is delayed past its estimated arrival date using a Linear Regression model?
